question: Which parts come out in Korean, which in English?
---

That split is fixed, and it follows one rule: what you use to plan and to talk to yourself is Korean, what happens in the classroom is English.

Korean output: the lesson plan (지도안), rule explanations, the assessment rubric, and the body of the parent notice.

English output: the teacher talk script, sentence scaffolds, the slide text students see, and the worksheet questions.

This is deliberate. Materials a student handles stay in the target language; everything that is really you talking to yourself stays in the language you think in.
