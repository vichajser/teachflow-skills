question: Where do the files go? Does anything leave my computer?
---

The skills write only to an `outputs/` folder inside the working directory you started them in. They do not write anywhere outside that directory.

They do not call external URLs, and they do not send your textbook or the files they produce anywhere. Run them with your machine offline and they behave the same.

One thing to be clear about: your agent and its model are a separate product from a separate company. Whatever you type into that agent — including your textbook — goes to whoever provides it, under their terms. That is true whether or not you use our skills.

You can check this yourself — [how to verify it](/en/security).
