question: Do I have to install all six?
---

No. Each skill is a separate folder and works on its own, so you can install only the ones you need.

We do suggest installing them together, though. `lesson-workflow` decides the unit split, the lesson flow and the slide outline, and the other five take that decision as their input. Without it you still get usable files, but they will not line up with one another the way they do when the whole set runs in order.
