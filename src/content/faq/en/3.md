question: Can I use it without a textbook?
---

`lesson-workflow` needs a textbook — it reads the unit and decides what the lesson teaches, so without source material there is nothing to start from.

The other five can run from your own lesson plan alone. Where a part genuinely needs the textbook text — the original slide wording in a slide deck, the example sentences in a vocabulary table — that part is left blank and marked "teacher to confirm" rather than filled in with something plausible.
