---
id: report-workflow
lang: en
title: Parent notice
tagline: Turns the finished lesson into a one-page A4 parent notice image.
inputs:
  - Lesson plan (required — the source of the topic, vocabulary, grammar and activities)
  - Textbook content (required — the accurate basis for the vocabulary and example sentences)
  - Class details (optional — class name, date, unit. Anything missing stays as a blank)
checks:
  - Item 4 (how the student took part) is left empty — the skill never writes it for you
  - No education jargon is left in ("scaffolding", "internalisation" and the like do not belong in a parent notice)
  - The vocabulary and example sentences match the textbook
  - No characters are broken or missing
---

The output is a **PNG** image filling one portrait A4 page (JPG on request), for sending home or
posting to a school notice feed.

| Item | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| 1. What we learned | In words a parent can follow, within three sentences |
| 2. Key vocabulary | 8–10 items, English and Korean side by side |
| 3. Grammar points | 1–2 points, with the textbook example sentences |
| 4. How the student took part | **Left blank.** A slot the teacher fills in by hand |
| 5. Suggestions for home | Two or three things a family can do at home |
| 6. What comes next | One sentence |

**Item 4 must stay empty.** It is only meaningful when the teacher writes down what they actually
observed. The remaining items cover the class as a whole — a class label and a date such as
`Class 3-2`, `15 October` (illustrative only) are the level of detail used.

**Personal data policy** — this skill does not put student names, student ID numbers, individual
grades, class rankings, photographs or contact details into the notice. Only class-level content
is written, in line with Korea's Personal Information Protection Act and the Ministry of
Education's school personal-data guidelines.

Even if the teacher explicitly asks for individual details to be added, the output guidance
carries a warning to check the school or institution's personal-data policy before sending
anything externally. Read that warning before you send.
