## This website

This is a static site. Pages are plain HTML, CSS and a small amount of JavaScript served
from our own server.

- **No analytics.** We run no analytics or measurement product of any kind.
- **No cookies.** The site sets no cookies and uses no local storage for tracking.
- **No third-party requests.** Everything a page needs is served from our own domain. There
  are no embedded scripts, pixels, iframes or hosted images, and no font, stylesheet or
  script is ever fetched from a CDN or any other company's server. We commit to keeping it
  that way: if this site ever uses a web font, we will serve it ourselves.
- **No advertising and no profiling.**

Some of our pages carry ordinary text links to other organisations — the ICO below, and
the statutes linked from our refund policy. A link is not a request: your browser
contacts those sites only if you click one, and from that point their own privacy
policies apply. Simply opening a page on this site contacts nobody but us.

Our web server keeps standard request logs — IP address, timestamp, requested path, user
agent — which exist to keep the server running and secure. Our lawful basis is legitimate
interest. They are not used to build a profile of you, and our policy is to retain them for
no longer than 30 days.

## The skills you buy

The skills are text files that run on your own computer, inside your own agent.

**Your textbooks, your lesson material and anything identifying your students stay on your
own machine.** They are never uploaded to us. We have no server that receives them, no
account to sync them to, and no telemetry in the package. The skills make no network
requests at all — you can run them with the machine offline and they work exactly the
same.

We therefore never see, store or process student personal data. There is no processing
agreement to sign with us, because we act as processor for nothing.

One thing to be clear about: your agent and its model are a separate product from a
separate company. Whatever you type into that agent goes to whoever provides it, under
their terms. That is true whether or not you use our skills. Check your agent's data
policy — and where your school requires it, use a setting or deployment that keeps
material local.

Our [security page](/en/security) sets out the same claims item by item, with the checks
you can run yourself to confirm them.

## When you email us

If you email us, we receive your email address, whatever you write, and any attachment
you send. We use it to answer you, and — if you buy directly — to issue your invoice and
send your download link. Our lawful basis is performance of a contract, or legitimate
interest in replying to an enquiry.

Please do not send us student names, photographs or schoolwork. We do not need them to
support you, and we would rather not hold them. If you send them by accident, tell us and
we will delete them.

Email lives in our mailbox with our email provider, which processes it on our behalf as a
service provider. We share it with nobody else, sell it to nobody, and send no marketing.

**Retention.** Correspondence that forms part of a sale — invoices, payment records and
the emails that document them — is kept for **six years** after the end of the accounting
period, as UK company and tax record-keeping rules require. Other correspondence is
deleted once the matter is closed and there is no longer a reason to keep it.

## When you buy

If you buy through our hosted checkout, Polar takes your payment as merchant of record
and is the controller of the data you give at checkout. Their privacy policy governs
that. We receive the order details we need to deliver your purchase — your email
address and what you bought — never your card details.

If you buy directly from us, we invoice you by email and take payment through a regulated
payment provider. **We never see or store your full card number** — the provider handles
it. We keep the invoice and the transaction reference for the six-year period above.

## Your rights

Under the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you, and get a copy
- **Rectification** — have inaccurate data corrected
- **Erasure** — have data deleted, except where we must keep it for tax records
- **Portability** — receive your data in a machine-readable form
- **Restriction** of, and **objection** to, processing based on legitimate interest
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, where processing rests on consent

To exercise any of these, email <crossxtop@gmail.com>. We respond within one month, as
the GDPR requires, and we do not charge for it.

## Transfers outside the UK and EEA

We are based in the United Kingdom. Our email and payment providers may process data
outside the UK or EEA, under the safeguards the GDPR requires for such transfers — an
adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses.

## Complaints

If you think we have handled your data wrongly, tell us first and we will try to put it
right. You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority.

In the UK that is the **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)** — <https://ico.org.uk>.
If you are in the EU, you may complain to the supervisory authority in your country of
residence.
